Tickover support

Last updated 04/11/2026

Tickover keeps your vehicles' MOT, tax and paperwork in one place: look up a car, van or motorbike by number plate, see its MOT and tax status and MOT history, get reminders before things run out, check clean air zones and ULEZ, and keep a business mileage log with HMRC rates.

Need help? Email us and we aim to reply within 2 working days. Please include your phone model and what you were doing. Don't send photos of your documents.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tickover an official DVLA or DVSA app?

No. Tickover is an independent app made by Kodfu. It shows public records from the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service and the DVSA MOT History API, but it is not a government service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DVLA, DVSA, HMRC, TfL or any council. To tax a vehicle, make a SORN or book an MOT, use GOV.UK.

Why does my number plate say "not found"?

Check the plate for typos (O and 0, I and 1). Brand-new registrations, recently changed cherished plates, some Northern Ireland, military, trade and very old vehicles may not be held in the DVLA or DVSA records we can see. You can still add the vehicle with Add without a plate and enter the dates yourself.

The lookup says the vehicle records service isn't responding

The DVLA or DVSA service is busy or down for maintenance, or lookups are temporarily switched off. Try again in a few minutes. Your saved vehicles and reminders keep working offline, and you can use Add without a plate in the meantime.

Why are MOT details missing or "unavailable right now"?

MOT history comes from a separate DVSA service. If it doesn't answer, Tickover still adds the vehicle with the DVLA tax and MOT dates and tries the MOT history again when you refresh. Vehicles under three years old (or historic vehicles) may not have any MOT tests yet.

Some details look wrong

Tickover shows what the official records say. If a date or detail is wrong at the source, it needs correcting there: for the V5C (log book) details see GOV.UK: change vehicle details; for an MOT record, contact the test centre that did the test. You can override MOT, tax and insurance dates on your own reminders in the app.

How do reminders work, and why didn't a notification arrive?

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work without a signal. Please check that notifications are allowed for Tickover in your phone's settings, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them, and on Android that battery optimisation isn't stopping Tickover. Phones limit how many reminders an app can schedule ahead, so open Tickover at least once a month and it will schedule the next ones. Reminders are a helper: you remain responsible for taxing, MOT-testing and insuring your vehicle on time.

Is the clean air zone checker final?

No. It's a guide based on fuel type, age and any Euro status on record. The official checker for each zone is the final word, and Tickover links to it.

Can I use the mileage log for my tax return?

The log uses HMRC's published approved mileage rates and exports a CSV you can keep with your records. Tickover isn't tax advice: check HMRC's current rates and rules or ask your accountant.

Does a free check tell me if a car is stolen, written off or on finance?

No. Public records don't include finance, write-offs or stolen markers. For a used-car purchase, use a paid history check from a specialist provider.

Is my data shared or sold?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your garage, reminders, logs and documents stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for the little we keep on our server.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Tickover Pro with, open Tickover, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the free trial or my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Tickover. Android: Google Play > profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Tickover. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are refunded by the store. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: request a refund on Google Play. Your statutory rights as a UK consumer are not affected.

How do I delete my data?

In Tickover go to Settings > Privacy > Delete everything. This deletes the anonymous device record and any watched plates on our server and all data on your phone. If you've already uninstalled the app, email us and we'll help.

Can I use Tickover outside the UK?

Tickover is for vehicles registered in Great Britain (with limited Northern Ireland coverage) and is available in the UK App Store and Google Play.
