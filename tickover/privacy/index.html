Tickover privacy policy

Last updated 04/11/2026

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your garage, reminders, logs and documents stay on your phone. To look up a vehicle we send its number plate to DVLA and DVSA through our server, and we keep only what we need to run the service, for as short a time as we can.

Who we are

Tickover is made by Kodfu Bilisim ("Kodfu", "we", "us"). For the personal data described here we are the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact us by email at gurkancaner@kodfu.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device and never sent to us: your vehicles and their nicknames, reminders, mileage trips (including places and purposes), fuel and cost entries, the documents vault (photos and PDFs, optionally locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint), insurance and breakdown details, accident and claim notes, and your settings. They are included in your own iCloud or Google device backup if you use one. Deleting the app deletes them.

We don't collect names, email addresses (unless you email us), phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. We never sell or share number plates or any other data with advertisers or data brokers, and we don't show ads.

Our lawful bases

  • Contract: vehicle lookups, watched plates and alerts are needed to give you the features you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention (rate limiting), keeping the service working and fixing faults.
  • Consent: notifications (you allow them in your phone's settings, and can turn them off any time).

Who receives data

  • DVLA and DVSA receive the number plate you look up, so they can return the public record. They are independent controllers of their own records.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts our server in the European Union (processor).
  • Apple and Google run the App Store and Google Play, process in-app purchases under their own privacy policies, and deliver notifications (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging) if you switch change alerts on.
  • GitHub hosts these web pages. We don't add any tracking to them.

Analytics and crash reporting are not active in this version of Tickover. If we add them later, analytics will be off unless you switch it on, will never include number plates, nicknames, places, free text or documents, and we will update this policy first.

International transfers

Our server is in the European Union, which the UK recognises as providing adequate protection. Apple and Google may process data in the United States under their own safeguards (such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. In Tickover, Settings > Privacy lets you export your data and Delete everything (server records for your device and all data on your phone). For anything else, email gurkancaner@kodfu.com; we reply within one month. Because we don't have accounts, we may ask for your device ID (shown in the app) to find your records.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Number plates and personal data

A number plate on its own identifies a vehicle, but it can become personal data when it is linked to a person. That's why lookups are cached without any link to your device, and plates you watch are treated as personal data and deleted when you stop watching or delete your data.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Your device's access token is kept in your phone's secure storage. IP addresses are only stored as salted hashes. Keys for the DVLA and DVSA services are kept on our server, never in the app.

Children

Tickover is not directed at children under 13. The store age rating describes the app's content only.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
