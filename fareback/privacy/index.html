FareBack privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short

  • There is no account. We never have your name, email address, postal address or a password on our server.
  • The details you enter for claim forms, your booking references, smartcard numbers and ticket photos stay on your phone.
  • Your journeys are checked on our server under a random ID, and deleted within 35 or 60 days of the travel date.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking.
  • You can export your data, and Delete all my data in the app removes it from our server straight away.

Who we are

FareBack is made by Kodfu Bilisim ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. For any question about privacy or your data, email gurkancaner@kodfu.com.

FareBack is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any train company, National Rail, Network Rail, Rail Delivery Group, Great British Railways or the Department for Transport.

What FareBack does with data

You save train journeys in the app. After the train arrives, our server checks the arrival time against National Rail data and works out whether the journey may qualify for compensation, with an estimated amount. FareBack then prepares a Claim Pack on your phone, and you submit the claim yourself on the train company's website. FareBack never submits claims and never acts on your behalf.

Your random ID

When you install FareBack, the app creates a random, pseudonymous ID and a secret. They are used only so the app can talk to our server securely. They are not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Apple or Google account. Data on our server is linked only to this random ID. Because it relates to you, we still treat it as personal data and protect it under the UK GDPR.

Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • the name, email address, phone number and address you enter for claim forms;
  • booking references;
  • smartcard numbers;
  • ticket photos (FareBack removes the photo's hidden details, known as EXIF data, such as location, and keeps ticket photos out of cloud backups);
  • claim statuses, payout amounts and your "money recovered" totals.

This data stays on your phone until you delete it, delete your data in the app or uninstall FareBack.

Data stored on our server

To check your journeys and send alerts, our server stores the following, linked only to your random ID:

  • Your random ID and secret. The secret is stored only in hashed form.
  • Journeys: station codes, date, times, train company, ticket type and any return leg.
  • Ticket price, only while the setting Show amounts in alerts is on. Turning the setting off deletes ticket prices from our server.
  • Check results: the delay and the estimated amount.
  • FareBack Pro features: your commute patterns and season ticket dates and price.
  • Settings: quiet hours, bank holiday region and alert options.
  • App details: the app version and platform (iOS or Android).
  • Whether FareBack Pro is active, as reported by the app. We receive no payment details.
  • Push notification token, only if server alerts are switched on, so we can send you alerts.
  • Rate-limit counters, to protect the service from abuse. These are keyed by a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, using a value that changes every day. We never store your IP address in its raw form.
  • Server logs, which we use to run and secure the service. They never contain secrets, tokens or ticket prices.

What we do not do

FareBack currently uses no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for marketing. If we ever add analytics or crash reporting, it will be optional and switched off unless you turn it on, and we will update this policy before it starts.

Who receives data

We share data only with the following, and only as described here:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts our server, in a data centre in the European Economic Area.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications, only when server alerts are switched on. It receives your push token and the text of the notification.
  • Apple and Google run the app stores and handle payments for FareBack Pro. We never see your card details. Their own privacy policies apply to your App Store and Google Play accounts.
  • GitHub hosts these web pages. The pages use no cookies, trackers or third-party scripts.

To check train times, our server queries train data providers (Rail Delivery Group and National Rail, through the Rail Data Marketplace). These queries contain only station codes, dates and times, never anything linked to you or your random ID.

Transfers outside the UK

Our server is in the European Economic Area, which is covered by UK adequacy regulations. Where Google or Apple process data outside the UK, they do so under their UK transfer safeguards: the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, or the UK-US data bridge.

Security

  • The app only talks to our server over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Your secret is stored on our server only as a SHA-256 hash.
  • The app keeps its key in the iPhone Keychain or the Android Keystore.
  • Server logs never contain secrets, tokens or ticket prices.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data. Most of them work directly in the app:

  • Access and portability: use the export in the app to get a copy of your data.
  • Erasure: go to Settings and tap Delete all my data. This deletes your data from our server straight away and wipes FareBack's data from your phone.
  • Rectification: edit your journeys and settings in the app.
  • Objection and restriction: change the relevant settings in the app, or email us.

You can also email gurkancaner@kodfu.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may need you to make some requests from the app itself. We reply within one month, as the law requires.

Deleting your data does not cancel a FareBack Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Children

FareBack is not directed at children under 13.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please email us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113.
