How much Merseyrail pays

The delay is measured at the station where your journey ends, against the timetable in force that day. The percentages are of the price you paid.

Arrival delaySingle ticketReturn ticket (one way delayed)
More than 30 minutes100% (full single)50% of the return

Merseyrail does not run Delay Repay. Its own scheme pays the full single or half a return when a journey entirely on Merseyrail is more than 30 minutes late. If your ticket also covers other train companies' lines, it pays 20% of the ticket price.

Example. A £4.00 single on a Merseyrail train that arrives 35 minutes late: the full £4.00 back.

More on the bands and how they compare: Delay Repay explained.

Does Merseyrail pay automatically?

No. Merseyrail has no automatic or one-click scheme on the pages we read, so you claim every delayed journey yourself.

How to claim from Merseyrail, step by step

  1. Note the train you planned to catch, its scheduled arrival and the time you really arrived. Claims are for the train you intended to use, even if you caught a later one.
  2. Check the delay against the table above, at the station where your journey ended.
  3. Get your ticket evidence ready (list below).
  4. Open Merseyrail’s claim form and enter the journey, the delay and your ticket.
  5. Choose how to be paid and submit. Keep the claim reference.
  6. Watch for the answer. If it is refused or less than you expected, challenge it (see below).

Postal claims: For paper season tickets: the refund application form to Freepost MERSEYRAIL CUSTOMER RELATIONS or any staffed station.

What you need

  • Proof of purchase, such as the confirmation email
  • Smartcard number or photocard ID for season and strike-day claims

How Merseyrail pays: Usually the same way you paid for the ticket, within 14 days of the claim being accepted.

Season tickets on Merseyrail

Merseyrail pays season tickets on average performance, not per journey: when the ticket ends, monthly and longer seasons can get 5% or 10% back if punctuality fell below its Passenger Charter targets. On a strike day, monthly and annual season holders who could not travel get a day's travel back, if they claim within 28 days of the strike.

Why Merseyrail claims get rejected

  • You gave the train you actually caught instead of the one you planned to catch. The National Rail Conditions of Travel (33.1) ask for the train you intended to use.
  • The claim arrived after the deadline.
  • The delay at your destination was under the threshold. It is measured against the timetable in force that day, so a published engineering-works timetable is the baseline, not the normal one (Conditions of Travel 32.1).
  • The ticket image does not show what the company needs, such as the ticket number, journey and price.
  • The journey was already claimed, including by an automatic claim.

If you think the decision is wrong, ask Merseyrail to review it with your evidence (Merseyrail complaints and contact). Then follow our step-by-step challenge guide (with a letter you can copy) and, if needed, the Rail Ombudsman.

Merseyrail Delay Repay questions

Is Merseyrail DR15 or DR30?

Merseyrail runs its own scheme instead of Delay Repay: it pays for delays of more than 30 minutes. The amounts are in the table above. Checked on Merseyrail’s own pages on 28/11/2026.

How long do I have to claim from Merseyrail?

Claim as soon as you can: Merseyrail's help pages we read give no deadline for delay claims, so we suggest claiming within 28 days, the general limit in the National Rail Conditions of Travel. Claiming within a few days is easiest: you still have the ticket and know which train you planned to catch.

Does Merseyrail pay compensation automatically?

No. You claim each delayed journey yourself.

I bought my ticket from another website. Who do I claim from?

From the train company whose train delayed you, not the website or app that sold the ticket. If your journey used several companies, you can send the claim to any of them and it must pass it on to the one responsible (National Rail Conditions of Travel 32.1). Refunds for journeys you did not make are different: those go to the retailer.

What if Merseyrail rejects or underpays my claim?

Ask Merseyrail to look again, with your evidence, then make a complaint. Train companies should usually give a full response within 20 working days. If you are still unhappy after its final response, or 40 working days have passed, take it to the Rail Ombudsman, which is free. See how to challenge a rejected claim.
