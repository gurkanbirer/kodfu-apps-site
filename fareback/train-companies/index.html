Not sure which company to claim from?

Claim from the company whose train delayed you, not the website or app that sold you the ticket. If your journey used several companies, you can send the claim to any of them, and it must pass it on to the one responsible (National Rail Conditions of Travel 32.1). Claim once per journey, not from each company. More in missed connections and split tickets.

The company is shown on the departure board and in journey planners. In FareBack, you pick the train and the app knows the company and its rules.
