Heathrow Express runs its own compensation scheme (Delay Compensation Scheme) instead of the standard Delay Repay bands. Claim within 28 days of the relevant journey.

How much Heathrow Express pays

The delay is measured at the station where your journey ends, against the timetable in force that day. The percentages are of the price you paid.

Arrival delaySingle ticketReturn ticket (one way delayed)
30 to 59 minutes25% of the price paid25% of the price paid
60 minutes or more50% of the price paid50% of the price paid

Heathrow Express does not run Delay Repay. Its own scheme in its Conditions of Carriage pays a share of the price paid for the Heathrow Express part of the journey, from 30 minutes late.

Example. A £25.00 ticket that arrives 40 minutes late pays 25%, £6.25; 65 minutes late pays 50%, £12.50.

Does Heathrow Express pay automatically?

No. Heathrow Express has no automatic or one-click scheme on the pages we read, so you claim every delayed journey yourself.

How to claim from Heathrow Express, step by step

  1. Note the train you planned to catch, its scheduled arrival and the time you really arrived. Claims are for the train you intended to use, even if you caught a later one.
  2. Check the delay against the table above, at the station where your journey ended.
  3. Get your ticket evidence ready (list below).
  4. Open Heathrow Express’s claim form and enter the journey, the delay and your ticket.
  5. Choose how to be paid and submit. Keep the claim reference.
  6. Watch for the answer. If it is refused or less than you expected, challenge it (see below).

Postal claims: Yes: the original ticket and a covering letter to Freepost HEATHROW EXPRESS Customer Relations Department.

What you need

  • A scan or photo of the ticket, or a copy with the journey details
  • Your contact details

How Heathrow Express pays: Credit or debit card only, normally the card you paid with.

Why Heathrow Express claims get rejected

  • You gave the train you actually caught instead of the one you planned to catch. The National Rail Conditions of Travel (33.1) ask for the train you intended to use.
  • The claim arrived after the deadline.
  • The delay at your destination was under the threshold. It is measured against the timetable in force that day, so a published engineering-works timetable is the baseline, not the normal one (Conditions of Travel 32.1).
  • The ticket image does not show what the company needs, such as the ticket number, journey and price.
  • The journey was already claimed, including by an automatic claim.

If you think the decision is wrong, ask Heathrow Express to review it with your evidence (Heathrow Express complaints and contact). Then follow our step-by-step challenge guide (with a letter you can copy) and, if needed, the Rail Ombudsman.

Good to know about Heathrow Express

  • Heathrow Express says you may claim under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 instead, but not both.
  • Complaints get a reply within 20 working days; after a final response or 40 working days you can go to the Rail Ombudsman.

Heathrow Express Delay Repay questions

Is Heathrow Express DR15 or DR30?

Heathrow Express runs its own scheme instead of Delay Repay: it pays for delays of 30 minutes or more. The amounts are in the table above. Checked on Heathrow Express’s own pages on 28/11/2026.

How long do I have to claim from Heathrow Express?

Claim within 28 days of the relevant journey. Claiming within a few days is easiest: you still have the ticket and know which train you planned to catch.

Does Heathrow Express pay compensation automatically?

No. You claim each delayed journey yourself.

I bought my ticket from another website. Who do I claim from?

From the train company whose train delayed you, not the website or app that sold the ticket. If your journey used several companies, you can send the claim to any of them and it must pass it on to the one responsible (National Rail Conditions of Travel 32.1). Refunds for journeys you did not make are different: those go to the retailer.

What if Heathrow Express rejects or underpays my claim?

Ask Heathrow Express to look again, with your evidence, then make a complaint. Train companies should usually give a full response within 20 working days. If you are still unhappy after its final response, or 40 working days have passed, take it to the Rail Ombudsman, which is free. See how to challenge a rejected claim.
