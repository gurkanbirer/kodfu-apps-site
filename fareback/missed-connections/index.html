Missed a connection? Claim for the delay at your final destination

If a late train makes you miss your connection, Delay Repay is worked out on how late you reach your final destination, not how late the first train was. A 10-minute delay that makes you miss an hourly connection can be worth your whole fare.

Checked 28/11/2026 against the National Rail Conditions of Travel (edition from 30/07/2026) and train company pages.

Measured at the end of the journey

Compensation is based on the delay in your arrival at the destination station, against the timetable in force that day (Conditions of Travel 32.1 and 33.1). Train companies say the same: Avanti West Coast and Caledonian Sleeper measure at your destination, not when you first set off, and Hull Trains bases it on the overall delay to your journey. ScotRail says that if you miss a connection because of a delay on a ScotRail train, you can claim for that too.

Example

You travel from Bath to Leeds, changing at Bristol, on a £60.00 single. The first train is 9 minutes late, you miss the connection, and the next train gets you to Leeds 62 minutes late. The claim is for a 62-minute delay at Leeds: on a DR15 or DR30 company, 100% of the single, £60.00. The 9 minutes on the first train do not matter on their own.

Who to claim from

  • The company responsible for the delay, usually the one whose late train made you miss the connection.
  • Not sure? Any company you used will do. Your claim can be made to any train company whose services you used, which must forward it to the one responsible (Conditions of Travel 32.1 and 33.2).
  • Once only. Claim the journey from one company, not from each. The GTR companies say you must only claim with the company responsible for the original delay.
  • Not the retailer. Delay Repay goes to the train company, not the website that sold the ticket.

Did you leave enough time to change?

You are expected to allow enough time to change trains. The Conditions of Travel (20) say the connection times in National Rail or a train company’s or retailer’s journey planner allow enough time, so a connection the journey planner gave you is safe. The GTR companies pay missed connections only when enough time was left and you hold a ticket for the entire journey.

If you are stranded

If disruption means you cannot finish your journey, the train companies will, where they reasonably can, arrange alternative transport to your destination or, if necessary, overnight accommodation (Conditions of Travel 28.2). Ask staff before booking a taxi or hotel yourself: self-arranged costs may not be refunded. After a cancellation you can normally use one of up to two trains before or after it with the same company (28.3), and if your train is likely to be more than 60 minutes late you may use your ticket on a later date with comparable restrictions (28.4).

Missed connection questions

Can I claim if my first train was only a few minutes late but I missed my connection?

Yes, if you reached your final destination late enough for your company’s threshold. The delay is measured at your destination, and you must have allowed enough time to change, such as a connection the journey planner gave you.

Which train company do I claim from after a missed connection?

The one whose train caused the delay. You can send it to any company you travelled with and it must forward it. Claim only once.

Do I give the train I planned to catch or the one I caught?

The trains you planned to catch, with the time you really arrived at your destination.
