2. What FareBack does

FareBack lets you save train journeys in Great Britain. After the train arrives, it checks the arrival time against National Rail data and shows whether the journey may qualify for compensation under the train company's Delay Repay scheme (or its own scheme), with an estimated amount. It then prepares a Claim Pack with copy buttons and a link to the train company's claim page. Until live train data is connected in the app, you can enter your arrival time yourself.

FareBack is a helper tool. You submit every claim yourself on the train company's own website. FareBack never submits claims, never acts on your behalf and never takes any share of your compensation.

3. Estimates only, no guarantee of payment

Every amount FareBack shows is an estimate, such as "about £4.20; the train company makes the final decision". It is based on the information you enter, train running data and our understanding of the train company's rules. The train company decides whether to pay and how much. We do not guarantee that any claim will succeed or that you will receive any particular amount.

4. Train data from third parties

Train times come from third-party sources (see Data sources and licences). This data can be late, incomplete or wrong, and we cannot check every record. Train companies' rules and claim pages can also change. Before you claim, check the details against your own experience of the journey and the train company's own information.

5. Your claims are your responsibility

  • You decide whether to claim, and you submit each claim yourself under the train company's own terms.
  • Only claim for journeys you actually made, and only once for each journey. Do not claim for a journey that has already been claimed or paid, including by an automatic compensation scheme.
  • Make sure everything you submit is true and accurate, including your arrival time.
  • Train companies may check claims and may investigate claims that look suspicious. False claims can have serious consequences.
  • Train companies set their own deadlines, usually 28 days from the date of travel. It is up to you to claim in time.
