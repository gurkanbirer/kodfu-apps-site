Free, with no sign-up and nothing saved. These 5 questions are original, written from the facts in the handbook. No app or website can show you the real test questions.

What this chapter covers

Chapter 2 explains what the United Kingdom actually is. The names are easy to mix up: the UK, Great Britain and the island of Ireland all mean different things, and these questions check that you can keep them apart.

  • The four countries of the UK and the country's full name.
  • What Great Britain means, and why Northern Ireland is part of the UK but not of Great Britain.
  • The capital cities, and Parliament at Westminster alongside the parliaments and assembly of Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
  • The Crown dependencies and overseas territories, which are linked to the UK but are not part of it.
  • The Union Flag, its three crosses, and why Wales does not appear on it.

This page has 5 questions rather than 10. We only publish questions that are free in the app, and only a small number are free for this chapter. One more is in the 10-question quiz on the main practice page.

  1. Question 1 · Choose one answer

    Which list names all the countries of the United Kingdom?

    1. England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland
    2. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
    3. England, Scotland and Wales
    4. England, Wales and Northern Ireland
    Show the answer

    Answer B: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

    The UK has four countries: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The Republic of Ireland is a separate, independent country.

    Source: Handbook ch. 2 · What is the UK?

  2. Question 2 · Choose one answer

    Which countries together make up Great Britain?

    1. England, Scotland and Northern Ireland
    2. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
    3. England, Scotland and Wales
    4. England and Wales only
    Show the answer

    Answer C: England, Scotland and Wales

    Great Britain means England, Scotland and Wales. Adding Northern Ireland gives the United Kingdom.

    Source: Handbook ch. 2 · What is the UK?

  3. Question 3 · Choose one answer

    What is the full official name of the UK?

    1. The Commonwealth of Great Britain and Ireland
    2. The United Kingdom of England, Scotland and Wales
    3. The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
    4. The United Kingdom of the British Isles and Ireland
    Show the answer

    Answer C: The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland

    The official name includes both Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

    Source: Handbook ch. 2 · What is the UK?

  4. Question 4 · True or false?

    Northern Ireland is part of Great Britain.

    1. True
    2. False
    Show the answer

    Answer B: False

    Northern Ireland is part of the United Kingdom, but Great Britain means only England, Scotland and Wales.

    Source: Handbook ch. 2 · What is the UK?

  5. Question 5 · True or false?

    The Republic of Ireland is part of the United Kingdom.

    1. True
    2. False
    Show the answer

    Answer B: False

    The Republic of Ireland is an independent country. Only Northern Ireland, on the same island, is part of the UK.

    Source: Handbook ch. 2 · What is the UK?

Worth remembering

  • Great Britain is England, Scotland and Wales. Add Northern Ireland and you have the United Kingdom.
  • The four capitals are London, Edinburgh, Cardiff and Belfast. London is also the capital of the whole UK.
  • The Channel Islands and the Isle of Man are Crown dependencies, with their own governments and many of their own laws.
  • St Helena and the Falkland Islands are examples of British overseas territories. They are linked to the UK but are not part of it.
  • The Union Flag combines the crosses of St George, St Andrew and St Patrick. Wales had already been united with England when the first flag was made in 1606, so it is not shown separately.
