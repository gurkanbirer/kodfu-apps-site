Free CITB HS&E test practice questions

114 free practice questions for the CITB Health, Safety and Environment Operatives test, by topic, each with the answer and an explanation.

Free, with no sign-up and nothing saved. Every question is original and comes from the HiVis app. No app or website can show you the real test questions.

The core health, safety and environment test for labourers, apprentices and most trades. It is the test most people need for a green, blue or gold CSCS card. The real test has 50 questions in 45 minutes. Read the HS&E test guide for the format, booking and what to bring.
