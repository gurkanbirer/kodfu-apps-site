Sparkwise privacy policy

Last updated 04/11/2026

In short: Sparkwise has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your study data stays on your phone. We cannot see it and we do not collect it.

Who we are

Sparkwise is made by Kodfu Bilisim ("we", "us"). For this app we are the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact: gurkancaner@kodfu.com.

Data stored only on your phone

To work, Sparkwise saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • the exam or exams you chose and your exam date
  • your answers, scores, mock test results and readiness score
  • bookmarks, mistakes to review and your study streak
  • which Amendment 4 changes you have read
  • your settings (reminder time, daily goal)
  • whether you have unlocked Sparkwise Pro

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is included in your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google) if you have backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. You can delete it at any time in Settings with Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

If you buy Sparkwise Pro, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. We never see your card details, name or address. The app asks the store whether this Apple ID or Google account has bought Pro, and remembers the answer on your phone. Apple and Google process your purchase under their own privacy policies.

Reminders

Study and test-day reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from a server and we do not know when or whether you receive them. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Content updates

Sparkwise may download updated question packs from our content server, which is a plain file host. The download sends no account, advertising or device identifier. Like any website, the host receives your IP address to deliver the file and may keep it in short-lived security logs (up to 30 days). We do not use these logs to identify you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app's content correct and secure.

Analytics, crash reports and advertising

This version of Sparkwise sends no usage statistics, no crash reports and no advertising identifiers, and it shows no ads. If a future version offers anonymous usage statistics or crash reports, they will be off unless you switch them on in Settings, and we will update this policy first, naming the provider and what is sent.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's built-in voice. The text is processed on your phone by Apple or Google's speech service under their policies; we receive nothing.

Links to other websites

Links such as official booking pages open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Sparkwise is made for adults and older teenagers preparing for an exam. It is not aimed at children under 13 and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data, to object to processing and to complain. Because your study data is only on your phone, you can see, change or delete it yourself at any time. We hold no copy, so we have nothing to send you or delete on our side, but you can always write to us at gurkancaner@kodfu.com and we will reply within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to help first.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sparkwise handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app. Material changes will also be shown in the app.

Sparkwise is an independent study app made by Kodfu Bilisim. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by City & Guilds, the IET, BSI, the JIB, the ECS or EAL.
