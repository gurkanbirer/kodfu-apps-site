Common questions

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Sparkwise questions are original practice questions written from public sources. They cover the same topics and the same style as the real exams. The ECS publishes its own revision guide, and the 18th Edition exam is open book with your copy of BS 7671.

Which edition of BS 7671 does Sparkwise follow?

BS 7671:2018+A4:2026 (Amendment 4), which the current City & Guilds 2382-26 exam is based on. The app says which version every question follows.

Are the calculators accurate?

They use the published formulas and show their working so you can check them. They are revision aids, not design software, so check real installations against your own copy of BS 7671.

Does Sparkwise Pro renew or charge me again?

No. Pro is a one-off payment that covers both exams. There is no subscription, and we do not charge again when the regulations change.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, open Sparkwise, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Your study progress is stored only on your old phone, so it does not move across.

I found a mistake in a question.

Thank you. Tap Report under the question in the app, or email us with the question number. We check every report and fix confirmed mistakes in the next content update.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every question, note and mock test is on your phone. You only need a connection to buy or restore Pro.

How do I turn reminders off?

In Sparkwise, go to Settings, Reminders. You can also turn off notifications for Sparkwise in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is only on your phone. In Sparkwise, go to Settings and tap Delete all my data, or delete the app. We hold no copy on any server.
