10 free solar PV and battery storage questions

Questions from across both exams, with the answer and a short explanation. They are original Sparkwise questions written from BS 7671, MCS and network connection standards and public guidance, not City & Guilds questions, and they never copy the IET Codes of Practice.

  1. Question 1 · Choose one answer

    Why are wet lead-acid batteries put in Class 8 when they are carried as dangerous goods?

    1. They contain corrosive sulphuric acid
    2. They give off flammable hydrogen gas
    3. They are classed as miscellaneous articles
    4. They contain toxic heavy-metal dust
    Show the answer

    Answer A: They contain corrosive sulphuric acid

    Class 8 covers corrosive substances, and a wet lead-acid battery holds sulphuric acid electrolyte (UN2794). Miscellaneous articles are Class 9, the class used for lithium batteries.

    Source: CDG 2009 (ADR) · C&G 2923-34 LO2 guidance

  2. Question 2 · Select TWO answers

    Which TWO are AC-side functions of a grid-connected PV inverter? Select TWO.

    1. Synchronising its output with grid voltage and frequency
    2. Keeping output harmonics and flicker within set limits
    3. Tracking the array's maximum-power point through the day
    4. Checking the array's insulation resistance before starting
    Show the answer

    Answer A: Synchronising its output with grid voltage and frequency, B: Keeping output harmonics and flicker within set limits

    Synchronising and keeping waveform quality within harmonic and flicker limits concern the AC output. Maximum power point tracking and insulation monitoring are DC-side functions.

    Source: C&G 2922-34 handbook LO3.1

  3. Question 3 · Choose one answer

    A developer plans G98 type-tested PV and batteries on 40 new houses on one estate. What does the DNO process require?

    1. An application before the systems are fitted
    2. A notification within 28 days of each house commissioning
    3. A G100 export limit fitted at every house
    4. No contact, as each house is under 16 A
    Show the answer

    Answer A: An application before the systems are fitted

    Many G98 units close together can affect the local network, so the multiple-premises process needs details agreed with the DNO before installation. The 28-day notify-after route is for a single premises.

    Source: ENA EREC G98 multiple premises

  4. Question 4 · Choose one answer

    What is the main safety reason for segregating the positive and negative parts inside a PV combiner box?

    1. To reduce the risk of a DC short-circuit and arc between them
    2. To allow the negative busbar to be bonded to the earth terminal
    3. To keep the box cool enough for its IP rating to remain valid
    4. To let the string fuses share a single common neutral link
    Show the answer

    Answer A: To reduce the risk of a DC short-circuit and arc between them

    Keeping positive and negative parts apart, often in separate sections, lowers the chance of a line-to-line fault and a sustained DC arc. The DC side is not normally earthed.

    Source: C&G 2922-34 handbook LO4.2.5

  5. Question 5 · Choose one answer

    A 3.68 kW inverter (16 A) feeds the consumer unit over 18 m of 4 mm² cable rated 11 mV/A/m. What is the voltage drop as a share of 230 V?

    1. About 1.4%, so above the 1% aim
    2. About 0.4%, so within the 1% aim
    3. About 3.2%, so above even 3%
    4. About 0.14%, so well within 1%
    Show the answer

    Answer A: About 1.4%, so above the 1% aim

    VD = 11 × 16 × 18 ÷ 1000 = 3.17 V. 3.17 ÷ 230 × 100 = 1.38%, so a larger cable is needed to meet the 1% aim. 3.2% wrongly reads the volts as a percentage.

    Worked solution. Formula: VD (V) = mV/A/m × I × L ÷ 1000; VD% = VD ÷ 230 × 100

    1. Voltage drop in volts: 11 × 16 × 18 = 3168 mV = 3.17 V
    2. As a percentage: 3.17 ÷ 230 × 100 = 1.38%
    3. Compare: 1.38% is above the 1% recommendation

    Common mistake: Reading 3.17 V as 3.2% or forgetting to divide mV by 1000.

    Source: C&G 2922-34 handbook LO4.4

  6. Question 6 · Select TWO answers

    Which TWO are advantages of AC coupling compared with DC coupling? Select TWO.

    1. The PV and battery inverters can be sized independently
    2. There is more choice over where the battery can be put
    3. Fewer conversion stages mean lower losses when storing
    4. There is no need for any inverter dedicated to the battery
    Show the answer

    Answer A: The PV and battery inverters can be sized independently, B: There is more choice over where the battery can be put

    AC coupling separates storage from generation, so each inverter is sized on its own and the battery can sit wherever an AC circuit can reach. Lower conversion losses are a DC-coupling advantage, and AC coupling needs a battery inverter.

    Source: C&G 2923-34 handbook LO3.1.1

  7. Question 7 · Choose one answer

    Compared with lithium-ion or lead-acid of the same capacity, a vented nickel-cadmium battery is:

    1. more expensive and bulkier
    2. cheaper and more compact
    3. cheaper but much bulkier
    4. compact but less tolerant of heat
    Show the answer

    Answer A: more expensive and bulkier

    Vented NiCd is relatively expensive and takes up more space than lithium or lead-acid batteries of equivalent capacity, which is why it is kept for specialist UPS duties.

    Source: C&G 2923-34 handbook LO3.1.4

  8. Question 8 · Choose one answer

    Which cellar would PAS 63100 rule out for a battery?

    1. A cellar with no access to the outside of the building
    2. A cellar with its own door straight out to the back garden
    3. A cellar that is dry and has a concrete floor slab
    4. A cellar that has a light and a socket-outlet fitted
    Show the answer

    Answer A: A cellar with no access to the outside of the building

    Cellars and basements without access to the outside are excluded, because a fire there is hard to reach and smoke has no way out except through the house.

    Source: PAS 63100:2024 6.5.5 i)

  9. Question 9 · Choose one answer

    A household uses 7.2 kWh between the evening and the next morning. The battery allows 90% depth of discharge. What is the smallest nominal capacity that covers this?

    1. 8.0 kWh
    2. 6.5 kWh
    3. 7.2 kWh
    4. 7.9 kWh
    Show the answer

    Answer A: 8.0 kWh

    Usable energy = nominal capacity × depth of discharge, so nominal = 7.2 ÷ 0.9 = 8.0 kWh. Multiplying by 0.9 or adding 10% gives the wrong answer.

    Worked solution. Formula: Nominal capacity = energy needed ÷ depth of discharge

    1. Energy needed: 7.2 kWh overnight
    2. Divide by DoD: 7.2 ÷ 0.9 = 8.0 kWh

    Common mistake: Multiplying by 0.9 (6.5 kWh) or adding 10% (7.9 kWh) instead of dividing by the depth of discharge.

    Source: C&G 2923-34 handbook LO4.2.2

  10. Question 10 · Choose one answer

    Which standard, together with BS 7671, sets the colour and alphanumeric identification of DC conductors?

    1. BS EN IEC 60445
    2. BS EN 62446-1
    3. BS EN 60529
    4. BS EN 62262
    Show the answer

    Answer A: BS EN IEC 60445

    BS EN IEC 60445 covers identification of conductors and terminals by colour and letters. BS EN 62446-1 is PV testing, BS EN 60529 the IP code and BS EN 62262 the IK code.

    Source: C&G 2923-34 handbook LO4.4

How to prepare

  • Know G98 and G99. Which applies, the 16 A per phase threshold for type-tested units and whether you notify after or apply before connecting are easy marks.
  • Practise the PV sums. Annual yield estimates, cable and device ratings on the DC side and voltage drop on the AC side all need a calculator and a method you trust.
  • Learn the battery safety rules. Where a battery may and may not go, and what labels the installation needs, run through the 2923 design questions.
  • Use your hour well. Two minutes a question is enough if you know where things are in your Code of Practice; tab it before the day.

FAQ

How many questions are in the 2922-34 solar PV exam?

The online test has 30 multiple-choice questions in 1 hour, with a pass mark of approximately 75%. The 2923-34 battery storage test has the same format. Each award also has a practical assignment.

Are the solar PV and battery exams open book?

Yes. For 2922-34 you can use the IET Code of Practice for Grid-connected Solar Photovoltaic Systems (2nd edition) and BS 7671; for 2923-34, the IET Code of Practice for Electrical Energy Storage Systems (3rd edition) and BS 7671. A non-programmable calculator is allowed.

Has 2922-34 replaced the old 2399 solar course?

City & Guilds introduced 2922-34 and 2923-34 in late 2024 as its current solar PV and energy storage awards. Check with your centre if you hold an older qualification and want to know whether you need the new one.
