A timing plan for 120 minutes

Two minutes a question sounds generous until you spend eight minutes hunting for one table. A simple three-pass plan keeps the clock on your side. Here is a worked example of how it can go:

Reading your mock score

The pass mark used in Sparkwise mocks is 36 out of 60 (60%). One pass by a mark or two tells you little: a few unlucky questions on the day can take that away. Most people find a margin more reassuring, for example scores in the low 40s or better on two or three different mocks in a row. Look at the shape of the result too:

  • Low marks in Parts 4 and 5: this is nearly half the exam. Go back to protection against electric shock, wiring systems and protective devices, earthing and bonding.
  • Calculations wrong but the method was close: practise cable and fault calculations with worked solutions until the formula and the table come together quickly.
  • Ran out of time: this is a book-navigation problem, not a knowledge problem. Practise finding regulations, and tab your book as described in the exam guide.
  • Amendment 4 questions dropped: read what changed in A4:2026, then the Amendment 4 practice questions.

What to do after each mock

  1. Review every wrong and flagged answer with the book open, and find the regulation or table that gives the answer, even if the explanation already says where it is.
  2. Practise the weakest two areas by topic before you sit another mock, rather than taking mock after mock.
  3. Retake the same numbered mock a week later to check the gap has closed, then try a different one.
  4. Sit your last mock in exam conditions: same book, a non-programmable calculator, phone notifications off, two hours in one go.

FAQ

Is there a free 18th Edition mock test?

Yes. Sparkwise includes one full 60-question, 2-hour 18th Edition mock for free, plus 109 free practice questions in the app. You can also try sample questions in your browser on our free practice pages. More mocks and every question come with Pro, £12.99 once.

Can I use 2382-22 mock exams to revise for 2382-26?

Only with care. Much of BS 7671 is unchanged, but 2382-22 was written for A2:2022, which was withdrawn on 15/10/2026. Questions on changed regulations, and anything new in Amendment 4, can teach you the wrong answer. Use mocks written for 2382-26 and BS 7671:2018+A4:2026.

How many mock tests should I do before the exam?

There is no set number. A common approach is one early mock to find your weak Parts, topic practice in between, and two or three later mocks under exam conditions until you are scoring with a comfortable margin above the pass mark.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Sparkwise questions are original, written from public sources on the same topics and in the same style as the real exam. They are not City & Guilds questions and Sparkwise is not affiliated with City & Guilds, the IET or BSI.

Do I need my BS 7671 book for the mock?

Yes, if you want the mock to tell you anything. The real exam is open book with BS 7671:2018+A4:2026, so practise with your own copy, tabbed the way you will take it in.
