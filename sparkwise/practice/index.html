18th Edition practice (BS 7671:2018+A4:2026)

The City & Guilds 2382-26 exam tests whether you can find and apply the requirements of the current Wiring Regulations. It is an online multiple-choice exam of 60 questions in 120 minutes, and it is open book, so practise with your own copy of BS 7671 to hand. Each answer points to a regulation, table or appendix to look up. Read the 18th Edition exam guide and what changed in Amendment 4.

  • Assessment of general characteristicsChapters 31 to 36: supplies and earthing systems, maximum demand, external influence codes, compatibility, maintainability, safety services and continuity of service. · 10 questions

ECS HS&E practice

The ECS Health, Safety and Environmental assessment is the test most electrotechnical workers pass to get or renew an ECS card. It has 50 multiple-choice questions in 30 minutes and is closed book. The CITB HS&E test used for CSCS cards does not count for ECS. Read the ECS HS&E assessment guide.

  • General health and safety at workHealth and safety law, duties, risk assessment, method statements, CDM 2015, permits and safety signs. · 10 questions
  • Manual handlingAvoiding, assessing and reducing lifting risks, safe lifting technique and handling aids. · 10 questions
  • Reporting accidentsRIDDOR, the accident book, near misses, dangerous occurrences and first-aid provision. · 10 questions
  • Personal protective equipmentWho provides and looks after PPE, choosing the right head, eye, hand, foot, hearing and breathing protection. · 10 questions
  • Health and hygieneCOSHH, dust, noise, vibration, skin problems, Weil's disease, welfare and fitness for work. · 10 questions
  • Fire and emergencyFire triangle, extinguishers, fire stopping and compartmentation, hot work, evacuation, first aid and electric shock. · 10 questions
  • Work at heightThe Work at Height Regulations, ladders, stepladders, scaffolds, mobile towers and MEWPs. · 10 questions
  • Work equipmentPUWER, hand and power tools, 110 V site supplies, cordless tools, abrasive wheels and lifting equipment. · 10 questions
  • Special site hazardsAsbestos, buried services, excavations, overhead lines, confined spaces and lone working. · 10 questions
  • Electrotechnical safetyElectricity at Work Regulations, safe isolation, test equipment, lock-off, live working, RCDs and solar PV. · 10 questions
  • EnvironmentalWaste duty of care, WEEE, hazardous waste, pollution, spills, nuisance and saving energy. · 10 questions
