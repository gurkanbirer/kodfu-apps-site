18th Edition practice (BS 7671:2018+A4:2026)

The City & Guilds 2382-26 exam tests whether you can find and apply the requirements of the current Wiring Regulations. It is one online multiple-choice exam of 60 questions in 120 minutes, with a pass mark of about 60%.

It is open book, so have your own copy of BS 7671 ready. Bookmarks, highlighting and short cross-reference notes are allowed, written notes are not, and you need a non-programmable calculator. Check the exact rules with your exam centre.

These 10 questions run across the book, from scope and definitions to protection, inspection and testing, special locations and a voltage drop calculation. Each answer points to a regulation, table or appendix to look up. We never reproduce BS 7671 text.

ECS HS&E practice

The ECS Health, Safety and Environmental assessment is the test most electrotechnical workers pass to get or renew an ECS card. It has 50 multiple-choice questions in 30 minutes, it is closed book, and you need 43 right (86%). The CITB HS&E test used for CSCS cards does not count for ECS.

These 10 questions are spread across the syllabus: general health and safety, manual handling, reporting accidents, PPE, fire, work at height, work equipment, asbestos, waste and safe isolation. They are written from legislation and HSE guidance, and are not taken from the ECS revision guide.
