The 18th Edition exam (2382-26)

How many questions are in the 18th Edition exam, and what is the pass mark?

The City & Guilds 2382-26 exam has 60 multiple-choice questions in 2 hours. City & Guilds gives the pass mark as about 60%; Sparkwise mocks use 36 out of 60. It is a single on-screen test, pass or fail. Full details are in our 18th Edition exam guide.

Is the 18th Edition exam open book?

Yes. You may use your own copy of BS 7671:2018+A4:2026 and nothing else. That makes knowing your way around the book as important as knowing the rules, so practise looking answers up rather than memorising them.

Can I write notes or use tabs in my BS 7671 book?

Tabs, bookmarks, highlighting and short cross-references (such as a table number next to a regulation) are allowed. Written notes, sample questions and extra diagrams are not. Your centre may check your book, so if yours is heavily marked, ask your centre before the day.

Which edition of the book do I need?

BS 7671:2018+A4:2026, the IET Wiring Regulations 18th Edition with Amendment 4, which has an orange cover. The previous version (A2:2022 with A3:2024) was withdrawn on 15/10/2026 and the exam is written against A4. See what changed in Amendment 4.

What calculator can I use?

A non-programmable calculator. Practise with the one you will take in, so you are not learning its keys during the exam.

How do I book the 18th Edition exam? Can I sit it at home?

You book through a City & Guilds approved centre, such as a college or training provider, not with City & Guilds directly. Many centres sell it with a short course, and some offer the exam only. Prices, dates and whether any part can be done remotely are set by the centre, so ask them.

I passed an older 18th Edition exam. Do I need to take 2382-26?

Your existing certificate still shows what you achieved. Whether you need the latest version depends on your employer, your clients or your registration scheme, so ask them. City & Guilds describes 2382-26 as being for practising electricians updating to the latest edition and for newly qualified electricians. The previous 2382-22 closed to new registrations on 31/07/2026.

Which parts of the exam carry the most marks?

Part 4 (protection for safety) and Part 5 (selection and erection) together account for about half of the 60 questions in the City & Guilds test specification. Part 8 and the appendices, Part 7 special locations and Part 3 general characteristics come next.

The ECS HS&E assessment

How many questions are in the ECS HS&E test, and what is the pass mark?

50 multiple-choice questions in 30 minutes, with one correct answer from four. You need 43 right (86%), so you can afford only seven mistakes. It is closed book. More in our ECS HS&E assessment guide.

Can I take the ECS health and safety test at home?

Yes. The ECS offers the assessment online with remote invigilation, so you can sit it at home or at work in a private space with a webcam, a microphone and a suitable connection. Face-to-face sessions are also available through selected employers and training providers. Follow the booking steps on the ECS website.

Does my CSCS (CITB) health and safety test count for an ECS card?

No. The ECS does not accept the CITB test used for CSCS cards. You need the ECS HS&E assessment itself, or a valid exemption.

How long is an ECS HS&E pass valid?

Two years. Within that time you need to apply for your ECS card, or you will have to sit a new assessment.

How much does the ECS HS&E assessment cost?

The ECS lists its fees on its website, and some providers add their own charge, so check the current price with the ECS or your provider. Do not pay a third-party booking agent: the ECS warns that they charge more than you need to pay.

What is the best way to revise for the ECS test?

Start with the official ECS revision book, which you can download from the ECS website. Then practise by topic, giving most time to fire and emergency (the biggest area), general health and safety, electrotechnical safety and work at height, and finish with timed 30-minute mocks until you score comfortably above 43.
