The online test format

2922-34 is the Level 3 award in installing small solar photovoltaic systems; 2923-34 is the Level 3 award in small electrical energy storage systems. Both are short courses for electricians who already hold a Level 3 electrotechnical qualification, and each has an online test (unit 301) and a practical assignment (unit 302). How to prepare

  • Know G98 and G99. Which applies, the 16 A per phase threshold for type-tested units and whether you notify after or apply before connecting are easy marks.
  • Practise the PV sums. Annual yield estimates, cable and device ratings on the DC side and voltage drop on the AC side all need a calculator and a method you trust.
  • Learn the battery safety rules. Where a battery may and may not go, and what labels the installation needs, run through the 2923 design questions.
  • Use your hour well. Two minutes a question is enough if you know where things are in your Code of Practice; tab it before the day.

FAQ

How many questions are in the 2922-34 solar PV exam?

The online test has 30 multiple-choice questions in 1 hour, with a pass mark of approximately 75%. The 2923-34 battery storage test has the same format. Each award also has a practical assignment.

Are the solar PV and battery exams open book?

Yes. For 2922-34 you can use the IET Code of Practice for Grid-connected Solar Photovoltaic Systems (2nd edition) and BS 7671; for 2923-34, the IET Code of Practice for Electrical Energy Storage Systems (3rd edition) and BS 7671. A non-programmable calculator is allowed.

Has 2922-34 replaced the old 2399 solar course?

City & Guilds introduced 2922-34 and 2923-34 in late 2024 as its current solar PV and energy storage awards. Check with your centre if you hold an older qualification and want to know whether you need the new one.
