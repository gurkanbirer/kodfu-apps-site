Sparkwise: 18th Edition exam and ECS test practice

Sparkwise is a revision app for UK electricians and apprentices. It helps you prepare for the two exams most electricians meet: the 18th Edition wiring regulations exam (City & Guilds 2382-26, based on BS 7671:2018+A4:2026) and the ECS Health, Safety and Environmental assessment you need for an ECS card.

It has 927 original practice questions with explanations, full timed mock exams in the same format as the real ones, and study notes for every topic. It works offline, has no account and no ads, and Pro is a single payment that covers both exams.

What's in the app

For the 18th Edition exam

  • Practice by Part of BS 7671: scope and definitions, general characteristics, protection for safety, selection and erection, inspection and testing, special locations, Part 8 and the appendices.
  • 60-question, 2-hour mock exams with an open-book reminder, a question navigator, flags and results grouped by Part.
  • Explanations that point you to the book. Each answer gives the regulation, table or appendix to look up. We never reproduce BS 7671 text, so you build the habit of finding it yourself.
  • Worked solutions for calculation questions (maximum Zs, voltage drop, rating factors, fault current, adiabatic checks), with the formula, each step and the usual mistake.
  • Maximum Zs calculator for Type B, C and D circuit-breakers, with the 80% rule and the working shown.
  • Amendment 4 hub: what changed in A4:2026, with questions on each change. See our Amendment 4 summary.
  • Find it fast drills: "where in BS 7671 would you look?" questions to speed up your open-book searching.

For the ECS HS&E assessment

  • All 11 syllabus areas, from general health and safety and manual handling to fire and emergency, work at height and electrotechnical safety.
  • 50-question, 30-minute mock tests with the same topic mix as the real assessment and the 43 out of 50 pass mark.
  • Questions written from health and safety law and HSE guidance, not copied from the ECS revision guide.

For both exams

  • Spaced review that brings back the questions you got wrong, plus mistakes and bookmarks lists.
  • A readiness score, a daily goal and an optional daily reminder.
  • Exam-day checklists for both exams.
  • Read aloud, dark mode and large text.
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, and your study data stays on your phone.

Email support

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Sparkwise questions are original practice questions written from public sources. They cover the same topics and the same style as the real exams. The ECS publishes its own revision guide, and the 18th Edition exam is open book with your copy of BS 7671. No app or website can show you the real exam questions.

Which edition of BS 7671 does Sparkwise follow?

BS 7671:2018+A4:2026 (Amendment 4), which the current City & Guilds 2382-26 exam is based on. The app says which version its 18th Edition questions follow.

Are the calculators accurate?

They use the published formulas and show their working so you can check them. They are revision aids, not design software, so check real installations against your own copy of BS 7671.

Does Sparkwise Pro renew or charge me again?

No. Pro is a one-off payment of £12.99 that covers both exams. There is no subscription, and we do not charge again when the regulations change.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you bought with, open Sparkwise, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Your study progress is stored only on your old phone, so it does not move across.

I found a mistake in a question. What should I do?

Thank you. Tap Report under the question in the app, which opens an email with the question number filled in, or email us with the question number. We check every report and fix confirmed mistakes in the next content update.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every question, note and mock test is on your phone. You only need a connection to buy or restore Pro.

How do I turn reminders off?

In Sparkwise, go to Settings, then Your plan, and switch off Daily reminder. You can also turn off notifications for Sparkwise in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is only on your phone. In Sparkwise, go to Settings and tap Reset progress under Your data. This deletes your answers, mock results, bookmarks, review schedule and plan, and keeps your Pro purchase. Deleting the app removes everything. We hold no copy on any server.

Can Sparkwise book my exam?

No. Sparkwise is not the official test and cannot book it for you. You book the 18th Edition exam through a City & Guilds approved centre, and the ECS HS&E assessment as the ECS website explains.
