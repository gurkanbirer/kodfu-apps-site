Tickover: MOT, tax and insurance reminders

Tickover keeps your vehicles' MOT, tax and insurance dates in one place and reminds you before each one runs out. Add every car, van and motorbike you look after, type in the dates, and let Tickover keep track. No account, no ads.

Last updated 26/11/2026

Good to know: Tickover does not look up number plates at the moment, so it does not show MOT history or check other vehicles. The dates come from you, and GOV.UK's free checks are one tap away in the app. If plate lookups become available, we will say so here.

How it works

  1. Add a vehicle. Give it a nickname, and add the make, fuel and year if you like.
  2. Enter the dates you know: MOT expiry, tax due date and insurance renewal. Not sure? Tickover opens GOV.UK's free Check MOT history and Check if a vehicle is taxed services in your browser, so you can read the date and type it in.
  3. Get reminders before each date runs out. Every date shows the days left and a clear status: Valid, Due soon or Expired.

New to MOT and tax rules? Read our guide to MOT and vehicle tax dates.

What else is in the app

  • Clean air zone and ULEZ checker: from the fuel type and year you enter, see whether your car or van is likely to be charged in the London ULEZ, the Congestion Charge zone and clean air or low emission zones elsewhere in the UK.
  • "I drove in a zone" reminders for zones and the Dartford Crossing, so you pay before the deadline.
  • Business mileage log with a running tax-year total at HMRC's approved mileage rates.
  • Fuel and cost log for every vehicle.
  • Help guides for breakdowns, accidents, warning lights, parking fines and a stolen vehicle. They work offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tickover an official DVLA or DVSA app?

No. Tickover is an independent app made by Kodfu. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DVLA, DVSA, HMRC, TfL or any council. To tax a vehicle, make a SORN or book an MOT, use GOV.UK.

Can Tickover look up my number plate?

Not at the moment. Tickover works with the dates you enter. If you type a plate, Tickover can't look it up and offers Add without a plate instead: you enter the MOT, tax and insurance dates yourself and get the same reminders. To find a date, use GOV.UK's free Check MOT history and Check if a vehicle is taxed services, or look at your last MOT certificate. If plate lookups become available, we will say so on this page and in the store listing.

Does Tickover show MOT history?

No. GOV.UK's free Check MOT history service shows past MOT results and advisories, and Tickover links to it. Vehicles under three years old (cars and motorcycles under four in Northern Ireland), and some historic vehicles, may not have had an MOT test yet.

A date or detail looks wrong

Tickover shows the dates you entered, and you can change MOT, tax and insurance dates on your own reminders in the app. If an official record is wrong, it needs correcting at the source: for the V5C (log book) details see GOV.UK: change vehicle details; for an MOT record, contact the test centre that did the test.

How do reminders work, and why didn't a notification arrive?

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work without a signal. Please check that notifications are allowed for Tickover in your phone's settings, that Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them, and that battery optimisation isn't stopping Tickover. Phones limit how many reminders an app can schedule ahead, so open Tickover at least once a month and it will schedule the next ones. Reminders are a helper: you remain responsible for taxing, MOT-testing and insuring your vehicle on time.

Is the clean air zone checker final?

No. It's a guide based on the fuel type and year you enter. The official checker for each zone is the final word, and Tickover links to it.

Can I use the mileage log for my tax return?

The log uses HMRC's published approved mileage rates and exports a CSV you can keep with your records. Tickover isn't tax advice: check HMRC's current rates and rules or ask your accountant.

Can Tickover tell me if a car is stolen, written off or on finance?

No. Tickover doesn't check vehicles, and public records don't include finance, write-offs or stolen markers. For a used-car purchase, use a paid history check from a specialist provider.

Is my data shared or sold?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your garage, reminders, logs and documents stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for the little we keep on our server.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you bought Tickover Pro with, open Tickover, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the free trial or my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On Android: Google Play > profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Tickover. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are refunded by the store: request a refund on Google Play. Your statutory rights as a UK consumer are not affected.

How do I delete my data?

In Tickover go to Settings > Privacy > Delete everything. This deletes the anonymous device record and any watched plates on our server and all data on your phone. If you've already uninstalled the app, email us and we'll help.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Tickover is on Google Play only for now.

Can I use Tickover outside the UK?

Tickover is made for UK drivers and is available on Google Play in the United Kingdom. Its guides, zone rules and mileage rates are UK ones. In Northern Ireland, MOT rules differ from Great Britain: see nidirect.
